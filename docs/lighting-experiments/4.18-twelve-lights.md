## Implementation summary

Per [§8.1 of the testing plan](../lighting-testing-plan.md#81-418--twelve-lights). Removed the 24 per-LED `PointLight` constructions in [`src/3d/Tower3DView.ts`](../../src/3d/Tower3DView.ts) `buildLeds` (12 ring inset lights, layers 0–2; 12 ledge/base corner lights, layers 3–5). The 12 seal accent `PointLight`s in [`src/3d/SealManager.ts`](../../src/3d/SealManager.ts) `buildSealBacklights` are unchanged. Total scene PointLight count when the bulk-lights gate is open: **12** (down from 36).

- [`src/3d/LedEffectAnimator.ts`](../../src/3d/LedEffectAnimator.ts): `LedRef.redLight` is now `THREE.PointLight | null`; `writeLed` guards the `redLight.intensity =` write with a null check.
- [`src/3d/Tower3DView.ts`](../../src/3d/Tower3DView.ts): `buildLeds` stores `redLight: null` per ref; `setBulkLightsVisible`, `applyLightingToScene`, and `dispose` guard the four remaining `redLight.*` accesses. Bulk-lights gate state moves from 0↔36 to 0↔12 — the prewarm machinery (`updateLightsGate` / `setBulkLightsVisible` / `prewarmLightPrograms`) is kept intact and pre-compiles both gate variants.
- [`tests/unit/Tower3DView.test.ts`](../../tests/unit/Tower3DView.test.ts): assertions previously on `redLight.intensity` / `redLight.visible` moved to `driver.v` and `isLightsGateOpen`. The bulk-lights-gate test was split: one variant verifies the gate flips with `accentLight: false` (default), another constructs the view with `accentLight: true` and asserts the 12 seal accent PointLights actually flip.

Proxy meshes (ledge + base layers, [`Tower3DView.ts`](../../src/3d/Tower3DView.ts)) and halo sprites are unchanged — those are separate visual paths owned by `LedRef.proxyMesh` / `haloSprite`. The 12 seal proxies + sprites in [`SealManager.ts`](../../src/3d/SealManager.ts) are also unchanged.

## Interpretation

**Pass — meets every gate from [§1 success criterion](../lighting-testing-plan.md#success-criterion-verbatim-from-lighting-alternativesmd-58) by a wide margin.**

- Sequence `frameMs.median` drop on Retina full-window: **−88.3%** (gate threshold: ≥60%). Exceeds the gate by ~28 absolute pp.
- Empty `frameMs.median` regression: **none** — exactly matches baseline (8.3 ms on both canvases).
- 1-LED `frameMs.median` regression: **none** — improved dramatically (Display: 74.1 → 8.3 ms; Retina: 140.2 → 16.6 ms). Per [§3 of lighting-alternatives.md](../lighting-alternatives.md#3-why-1-led-on-costs-the-same-as-all-leds-on-bulk-lights-gate-anatomy) the predicted 12/36 ratio implies ~3× speedup; observed ratio at Retina is **~8.5×** (140.2 → 16.6), better than predicted because the 36-light fragment cost was already past the v-sync ceiling at this canvas.
- Programs stable across 3 sequence-5 transitions: **yes** — 30 throughout. Prewarm works correctly for the new 12-light gate state.
- Visual delta risk: not flagged; tradeoff (loss of ledge/base spill from corner-positioned PointLights) is documented and accepted per [§4.18 of the alternatives doc](../lighting-alternatives.md#418-consolidated-12-lights-design-one-pointlight-per-seal-ledgebase-as-emissive-dots).

This is a strong winning candidate by the perf criterion alone. Decision-time consideration: the implementation footprint is minimal (3 files, 184 lines of churn including comments), and the change is fully reversible. 4.18 should be the leader in [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) until/unless a Path B alternative produces a meaningful visual improvement at comparable perf.

## Notable observations / risks

- **No ambiguity-rule recaptures needed.** Every active-scenario delta vs baseline was far outside the ±5% noise envelope (>87% improvement in every case). The Empty window exactly matched baseline (8.3 ms median). Single-capture confidence is sufficient.
- **Sequence-5 snapshot's `drivers.ledsActive` and `visibleBloomMeshes` vary.** Baseline captured `ledsActive: 12` at Display and `4` at Retina; this run captured `7` and `6`. These are end-of-window snapshots of a dynamic sequence (`angryStrobe01`) — the value depends on which frame of the sequence the snapshot lands on. Not a regression; orthogonal to the perf measurement.
- **The 12 PointLights now in the shader hash are the seal accent lights (SealManager.buildSealBacklights).** With `accentLight` enabled (the default), these are made `visible: true` by the bulk-lights gate when any LED is active. The hash-stable count is **12** (post-gate-open), not 0 + the proxy meshes — those proxies have no light contribution, only emissive bloom. The bake-off's downstream alternatives can chain on top of this: 4.5 (LightProbe) would drop those 12; 4.4 (two directional) would replace them; 4.16 (emissive-standard) could combine with 4.18 to preserve the look while picking up the perf win.
- **Display canvas hit v-sync at 120 Hz across all scenarios.** The active-scenario windows show identical `frameMs.median = 8.3` to Empty, meaning the GPU has substantial headroom even with bloom enabled. The next bottleneck at the Display canvas size is not in the lights loop.
- **Retina full-window's `frameMs.max` of 25–27 ms in active scenarios.** A few longer frames (well under 1% of the sample) push the max above v-sync. p95 is at 17.4–17.9 ms — solidly within v-sync. The max outliers are likely GC + GSAP tick jitter, unrelated to the lights change.
- **chrome-devtools-mcp browser parity preserved.** Same browser, same machine, same canvas-size methodology as the baseline. The 2% canvas-area difference at Retina is dominated by the layout panel collapsing to slightly different heights between sessions — fragment-cost-per-pixel is the comparable axis.
- **No impact on the ledge/base LED visuals themselves.** Proxy spheres + halo sprites still render through the bloom pipeline, so the "LED bulb" appearance is preserved. Only the cast lighting onto surrounding drum surfaces is lost — and per [§4.18 of the alternatives doc](../lighting-alternatives.md#418-consolidated-12-lights-design-one-pointlight-per-seal-ledgebase-as-emissive-dots), those corner-positioned PointLights cast mostly outward (away from the drum interior) anyway.
